DeskPort for iPhone and iPad — Privacy Policy Effective: 2026-09-15

DeskPort is a remote desktop client. It connects to computers you authorize. It does not require a DeskPort account and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Remote sessions The host sends screen images, audio and connection information to your device. Your device sends keyboard, pointer and touch input to the host. These are processed to operate the session; DeskPort does not upload session content to a developer-operated server. Only connect to hosts you trust. Network security depends on the host, protocol and network configuration; not every connection or media channel is guaranteed to be encrypted.

Data stored on your device Saved host names, addresses, pairing/binding credentials, certificates and preferences are stored locally. Remove a saved host from Devices to remove its saved connection. Removing a host does not revoke authorization on that computer: also revoke the client in the host settings. Deleting the app removes its app-container data; operating-system backups and data already on a host are managed separately.

Network discovery and third parties Local-network permission enables host discovery and connections. The inherited Moonlight discovery implementation may query stun.moonlight-stream.org to discover a public IPv4 address. That service receives the network address and technical request data, not desktop video, audio or keyboard content. The service is operated independently of DeskPort. Network providers and authorized hosts also process connection traffic.

Permissions Local network: discover and connect to hosts. Bluetooth: optional Citrix X1 mouse support. Standard system-supported keyboards and mice use iPadOS input support. The app does not request access to your photo library, microphone, contacts or location.

Diagnostics and support The app can write technical connection diagnostics locally. Apple may provide crash reports according to your device settings. If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to investigate your request. Do not post passwords, pairing tokens, public/private addresses, personal screenshots or employer information in public issues.

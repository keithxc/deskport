Your desktop, on your iPad.

Use your own computer from iPad or iPhone with touch, keyboard and pointer controls. DeskPort brings your desktop applications and workspace to your mobile screen.

Start with DeskPort on your computer

The desktop app is free and open source. Install a host-capable DeskPort package on your computer, enable Sharing, then connect with the iPad/iPhone app.

Download DeskPort 0.7.3 for your computer on GitHub →
macOS Apple Silicon · Linux x86_64 · Windows x64 — viewer and host. The Linux Flatpak is viewer-only. Android client in closed testing (details).

DeskPort for iPad & iPhone on the App Store · Setup guide →

Built for desktop work

  • Saved computers with individual picture, audio and input preferences.
  • Direct touch or trackpad mode, plus an on-screen keyboard with modifier and navigation keys.
  • Portrait and landscape viewing, with desktop sizing options supported by your DeskPort host.
  • Connection progress, session controls and confirmation before taking over an active session.

The iPad/iPhone app requires a DeskPort desktop host. Standalone, unmodified Sunshine hosts are not supported. No DeskPort account or hosted cloud computer is included. Available display and input features depend on your computer, host package and connected devices.

Connect to your DeskPort computer

The iPad and iPhone app (and the Android client, currently in closed testing) require DeskPort on your computer. Standalone, unmodified Sunshine hosts and legacy Sunshine PIN pairing are not supported.

1. Install the free desktop app

Download DeskPort desktop from GitHub →

Choose a package for your computer and read its release notes and system requirements. Use a host-capable package: the Linux Flatpak is client-only and cannot share a desktop. Open DeskPort, enable Sharing, grant the permissions it requests for sharing, and keep the computer awake.

2. Connect and approve

  1. For initial setup, put your computer and mobile device on the same local network.
  2. Allow Local Network access on iPad/iPhone. Select the DeskPort computer card, or add a reachable address manually.
  3. Approve the device request in DeskPort on the computer.
  4. Open the paired computer to enter its desktop. If another client is connected, review the takeover prompt before confirming.

Work with your desktop

Use direct touch gestures or select Trackpad mode in the device settings. The on-screen keyboard offers modifier and navigation keys. Each saved computer has its own picture, audio, input and desktop preferences. Desktop sizing depends on host capabilities. Session controls let you disconnect without shutting down the computer.

Cannot find or connect?

Confirm DeskPort is running with Sharing enabled on the computer. Check Local Network permission, the firewall, the saved address and whether the devices can reach each other. Guest Wi-Fi may isolate devices. Install a current DeskPort desktop release if the client reports missing host capabilities. Installing standalone Sunshine is not a substitute for DeskPort.

For access away from home, use a private network or VPN you control that makes both devices reachable. DeskPort does not provide a relay or VPN service. Do not expose host ports to the internet as a troubleshooting shortcut.

Android (closed testing)

DeskPort for Android is in Google Play closed testing and is not yet publicly listed. New Google Play developer accounts must run a closed test with testers opted in for 14 consecutive days before applying for production, and that requirement has not been met yet. Closed-test installs are still a paid download on Google Play. A free claiming period ran Sep 25–Oct 8, 2026; Google Play requires a waiting period before the next one, so the next free period is expected next month. Redemption codes do not work yet. If Play shows a price, please wait rather than pay.

How to join: join the tester group, then with the same Google account become a tester.

中文快速开始

  1. 从 GitHub 下载免费开源的 DeskPort 电脑端，按发布说明选择适合电脑系统、支持共享的安装包。Linux Flatpak 仅能作为客户端，不能共享电脑桌面。
  2. 打开电脑端 DeskPort，开启「共享」，按提示授予共享所需权限，并保持电脑唤醒。
  3. 首次连接让移动设备与电脑处于同一本地网络；允许移动端访问本地网络，点击电脑卡片或手动添加可达地址。
  4. 在电脑端 DeskPort 批准设备请求，再从移动设备打开桌面。已有其他客户端连接时，先确认是否接管会话。

仅支持 DeskPort 电脑端，不支持原版 Sunshine，也无需到 Sunshine 网页输入传统 PIN。可以为每台电脑设置画面、声音、输入和桌面偏好，使用直接触控、触控板模式或屏幕键盘。桌面尺寸能力取决于电脑端；断开连接不会关闭电脑。

找不到电脑时，检查电脑端共享开关、本地网络权限、防火墙、保存的地址和访客 Wi-Fi 隔离。提示缺少主机能力时，请更新 DeskPort 电脑端。外网连接需要自行配置两端可达的私有网络或 VPN；DeskPort 不提供中继或 VPN 服务。

DeskPort Android 版正在 Google Play 封闭测试中，尚未公开上架。Google Play 要求新开发者账号先进行封闭测试，测试者需连续参与满 14 天才能申请正式上架，目前还没有达到这个要求。封闭测试期间，在 Google Play 安装仍然需要付费。上一轮限时免费已于 2026 年 9 月 25 日至 10 月 8 日结束；Google Play 要求两次限免之间间隔一段时间，下一次免费预计下个月开放。兑换码目前还不能使用。如果 Play 上显示价格，请先等待，不要付费。

加入方式：先加入测试者群组，再用同一个 Google 账号成为测试者。

Support

Report a problem through GitHub Issues. Include the client and desktop versions, operating systems, device model and reproduction steps. Issues are public: remove credentials, private addresses and personal content.
